Ad Valorem Court Fees (Percentage of Claim)

In civil cases, court fee is often a percentage of the claim amount:

  • Andhra Pradesh/Telangana: 7.5% of claim value
  • Delhi: 4-6% of claim value (capped)
  • Maharashtra: 2-5% of claim value
  • Tamil Nadu: 7.5% of claim value
  • Karnataka: 3-5% of claim value
  • West Bengal: 5-7.5% of claim value

How to Get Free Legal Help

  1. Call 15100 - Free legal advice for everyone
  2. Visit District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) - Free lawyer if eligible
  3. Women: Free legal aid in all cases - call 1091 or 15100
  4. SC/ST: Free legal aid under Protection of Civil Rights Act
  5. Low Income: Below ₹1 lakh/year - free legal aid
  6. PIL: Public Interest Litigation - minimal court fees

Warning: Avoid Touts

Never pay money to touts outside court who promise quick judgments. Always verify:

  • Check advocate's Bar Council enrollment
  • Get written fee agreement
  • Use only official court fee counters
  • Legal aid lawyers are free - never pay them
